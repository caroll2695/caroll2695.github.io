About

I'm Head of Engineering, Edge and Platforms at Galaxy. I lead a 30-person engineering organization that builds custody, staking, tokenization and DeFi solutions for tier-one banks and asset managers, with globally distributed smart contract and back-end teams across multiple time zones.

I came up through site reliability and platform engineering, running the infrastructure behind Galaxy's trading business, then built and launched the staking platform. Today I also lead forward-deployed engineering, where our engineers work on-site with clients.
